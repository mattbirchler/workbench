SEE YOUR DAY
There's a new Overview of the day your agents have had. Tap the chart button at the top of the project list. It shows four totals for the day: how long agents worked, how many prompts you sent, the lines they wrote and removed, and how long they waited on you.

Under that is a timeline with a row for each project. Bars show when its agents were working, amber shows when they were stopped on a question for you, and a green dot marks where work finished. Below the timeline are the latest things that finished or needed you, and where the time went by project.

Switch between Today and Yesterday at the top, and tap a project to open it.

The Overview needs Workbench 0.10.3 or later on your Mac. The button appears once your Mac has it.
